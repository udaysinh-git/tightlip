# Reporting a vulnerability

Email [security@kurolabs.in](mailto:security@kurolabs.in). Describe the affected
release or commit, where you observed the issue, and how we can confirm it
using synthetic data. Keep share links, keys, passwords and other people's
data out of reports.

Give us time to investigate and deploy a fix before publishing details. Use a
local deployment for tests that spend attempts, consume resources or change
state. The deployment contact is also published at
`/.well-known/security.txt`.
